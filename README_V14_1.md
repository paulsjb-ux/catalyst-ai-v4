# Catalyst AI v14.1 — Research Diagnostics

v14.1 keeps the v14 adaptive trading logic unchanged and adds four evidence tools:

- adaptive restriction/rejection diagnostics;
- stress-test decomposition for costs versus delayed entry;
- confidence-component attribution for winners and losers;
- confidence calibration against observed win rates.

The Validation Centre and PDF/JSON evidence exports include all four sections.
These diagnostics are descriptive and do not claim causality or improved profitability.
