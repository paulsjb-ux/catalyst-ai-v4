# Catalyst AI v8.1.0 — Today’s Decision

Version 8 adds a simplified front door to the existing Catalyst trading desk.

## New

- **Today’s Decision** is the default opening page.
- Converts the latest routine into one conservative outcome: **TRADE**, **WATCH**, or **NO TRADE**.
- Shows market posture, confidence, BUY/WATCH counts and the leading opportunity.
- Explains the main reasons behind the decision in plain English.
- One-click access to Daily Routine and Executive Dashboard.

## Decision guardrails

- TRADE requires a genuine BUY, a score of at least 75, acceptable risk/reward and a non-defensive market.
- BUY ideas in a RISK OFF market are downgraded to WATCH.
- No BUY or WATCH candidates produces NO TRADE.
- The app remains market-intelligence only and does not execute trades.
