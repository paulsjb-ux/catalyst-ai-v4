# Catalyst AI v10.0 — One-Button Swing Desk

v10.0 makes the Daily Routine the default operating screen.

## Daily workflow

Press **Run Daily Routine** once. Catalyst updates market data, detects the regime, scans the universe, creates risk plans, applies proof-based swing filters, refreshes alerts and exports the result.

## Swing focus

- Fewer, higher-quality swing opportunities rather than more trades.
- Uses the strongest validated score band from the latest proof report.
- Gives priority to tickers with at least 30 completed trades and profit factor of at least 1.20.
- Requires trend, medium-term momentum and risk/reward confirmation.
- Prevents qualified entries in a defensive/RISK_OFF regime.
- Caps every new position at REDUCED size (15% maximum).
- Recommends no more than two new positions per routine.

The supplied v9.2.1 proof report is included as the starting validation baseline. Future proof reports can replace `storage/validation/latest_proof_report.json`.
